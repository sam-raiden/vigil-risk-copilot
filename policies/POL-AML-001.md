# POL-AML-001 — Structuring & Smurfing Detection

**Owner:** Financial Intelligence Unit | **Version:** 1.0 | **Applies to:** All deposit and cash channels

## Clause 1 — Purpose
1.1 This policy sets how the Bank detects and escalates deposits structured to avoid the cash reporting threshold.

## Clause 2 — Reporting Threshold
2.1 The cash transaction reporting threshold is INR 2,00,000 (₹2L) per transaction.
2.2 Deposits at or above the threshold are reported through the Cash Transaction Report process and are not treated as structuring on that basis alone.

## Clause 3 — Structuring Pattern Definition
3.1 Structuring is presumed where an account receives **three (3) or more cash deposits each between ₹1,80,000 and ₹1,99,999 within any rolling ten (10) day window**.
3.2 The pattern applies per account. Deposits through different channels (BRANCH, ATM, MOBILE, ONLINE) are aggregated.

## Clause 4 — Escalation
4.1 A structuring alert must be escalated to the Compliance Officer within **24 hours** of detection.
4.2 The alert must stay open until a documented disposition is recorded.

## Clause 4.5 — RING_PATTERN: Coordinated Activity Across Accounts
4.5.1 Three (3) or more distinct accounts transacting with the **same counterparty or device within a 72-hour window**, absent a legitimate shared business relationship, are presumptively treated as coordinated activity requiring escalation under Clause 4.1.
4.5.2 A legitimate shared business relationship includes widely used utility, tax, payroll or merchant counterparties paying or paid by many unrelated customers. Such counterparties are excluded from ring treatment.
4.5.3 Each account in a ring is individually reviewed; the ring finding is documented as one case that references all member accounts and transaction IDs.

## Clause 5 — Evidentiary Standard
5.1 A finding must cite the account ID, each transaction ID forming the pattern, the amounts, and the dates.
5.2 Findings that cannot be tied to specific transaction IDs must not be recorded as confirmed.

## Clause 6 — Record Retention
6.1 Structuring alerts, supporting transaction records and the documented disposition must be retained for at least five (5) years from the date of the transaction or closure of the account, whichever is later.
