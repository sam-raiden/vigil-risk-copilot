# POL-AML-002 — High-Risk Jurisdiction Transactions

**Owner:** Financial Intelligence Unit | **Version:** 1.0

## Clause 1 — Purpose
1.1 This policy governs transactions to or from jurisdictions identified by the Financial Action Task Force (FATF) as high-risk.

## Clause 2 — Definition
2.1 A high-risk jurisdiction is any country on the FATF "black list" (call for action) or "grey list" (increased monitoring), as maintained in the Bank's Jurisdiction Register.
2.2 For this Bank's current register, this includes Myanmar, North Korea, Iran and Panama.

## Clause 3 — Enhanced Due Diligence
3.1 Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) is **mandatory** for any remittance or transfer of **₹5,00,000 (₹5L) or more** to or from a high-risk jurisdiction.
3.2 EDD requires documented purpose of payment, beneficiary verification, and source of funds.
3.3 A transaction above the threshold with no EDD record on file is **non-compliant**.

## Clause 4 — Escalation
4.1 Every transaction under Clause 3.1 must be escalated to the Compliance Officer on the same working day, whether or not EDD is completed.

## Clause 5 — Evidentiary Standard
5.1 A finding must cite the transaction ID, amount, counterparty country and date, and state whether an EDD record exists.

## Clause 6 — Record Retention
6.1 Jurisdiction screening results, EDD files and escalation records must be retained for at least five (5) years from the date of the transaction.
