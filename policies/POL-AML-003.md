# POL-AML-003 — Transaction Velocity Monitoring

**Owner:** Financial Intelligence Unit | **Version:** 1.0

## Clause 1 — Purpose
1.1 This policy addresses rapid pass-through of funds, a common layering indicator.

## Clause 2 — Velocity Pattern Definition
2.1 A velocity pattern exists where an account receives a deposit of **₹5,00,000 or more** and **80% or more** of that amount leaves the account by withdrawal or transfer **within 24 hours** of the deposit.

## Clause 3 — Automatic Hold
3.1 On detection, the Bank may place an automatic hold on further outward movement from the account pending review.
3.2 A hold must be reviewed by the Compliance Officer within 24 hours.

## Clause 4 — Escalation
4.1 A velocity alert must be escalated to the Compliance Officer within **24 hours** of detection.

## Clause 5 — Evidentiary Standard
5.1 A finding must cite the account ID, the deposit transaction ID and amount, each outgoing transaction ID and amount, the elapsed hours, and the computed percentage moved out.

## Clause 6 — Record Retention
6.1 Velocity alerts, hold decisions and the related transaction records must be retained for at least five (5) years from the date of the transaction.
