# POL-AML-004 — STR Filing Timelines & KYC Obligations

**Owner:** Principal Officer (PMLA) | **Version:** 1.0

## Clause 1 — Purpose
1.1 This policy sets timelines for filing a Suspicious Transaction Report (STR) with FIU-IND and KYC dependencies.

## Clause 2 — STR Filing Timeline
2.1 Under Rule 8 of the PMLA Rules, an STR must be filed within **seven (7) working days** of the Principal Officer being satisfied that a transaction is suspicious.
2.2 Filing is always performed and signed off by a human Principal Officer. No system files an STR automatically.

## Clause 3 — KYC Dependency
3.1 Where the customer's KYC status is PENDING or EXPIRED, the case must be marked as a KYC control deficiency and is escalated alongside the STR assessment.
3.2 KYC must be refreshed before further high-value activity is permitted on the account.

## Clause 4 — Risk-Segment Escalation Multiplier
4.1 Cases on HIGH risk-segment customers are reviewed with priority: the internal review deadline is halved.
4.2 Cases on MEDIUM risk-segment customers follow the standard deadline.

## Clause 5 — Control Deficiency Reporting
5.1 Any missing EDD, expired KYC or missed escalation timeline must be recorded as a control deficiency and reported to the Compliance Committee at the next meeting.

## Clause 6 — Record Retention
6.1 STR copies, acknowledgements and KYC records must be retained for at least five (5) years after the end of the business relationship.
