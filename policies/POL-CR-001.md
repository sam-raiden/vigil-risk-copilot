# POL-CR-001 — NPA Classification & Provisioning

**Owner:** Credit Risk | **Version:** 1.0

## Clause 1 — Purpose
1.1 This policy sets Non-Performing Asset (NPA) classification and provisioning in line with RBI IRAC norms.

## Clause 2 — NPA Threshold
2.1 A loan account is classified as an NPA when interest or principal is overdue for **more than 90 days**.

## Clause 3 — Sub-classification
3.1 **Substandard:** NPA for up to 12 months.
3.2 **Doubtful:** NPA for more than 12 months.
3.3 **Loss:** asset identified as uncollectible.

## Clause 4 — Provisioning
4.1 Substandard assets: provision of **15%** of the outstanding amount.
4.2 Doubtful assets: provision of **25% to 100%** of the outstanding amount depending on the age of the doubtful classification.
4.3 Loss assets: provision of **100%** of the outstanding amount.

## Clause 5 — Evidentiary Standard
5.1 A finding must cite the loan account ID, days past due, outstanding amount, the sub-classification, and the provision computed with the percentage applied.
