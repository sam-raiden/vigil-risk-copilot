# POL-LIQ-001 — Basel III Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR) Compliance

**Owner:** Treasury & ALM | **Version:** 1.0

## Clause 1 — Purpose
1.1 This policy defines the Bank's LCR requirement and breach handling.

## Clause 2 — Requirement
2.1 LCR = High Quality Liquid Assets (HQLA) ÷ Total Net Cash Outflows over the next 30 days.
2.2 The Bank must maintain an LCR of **100% or more** at all times.

## Clause 3 — Breach Classification and Escalation
3.1 Any daily LCR below 100% is a **breach**.
3.2 A breach must be escalated to the Asset-Liability Committee (ALCO) within **4 hours** of detection.

## Clause 4 — Remediation
4.1 A remediation plan must be documented, stating the shortfall in HQLA, the actions taken, and the date the LCR is restored to 100% or more.

## Clause 5 — Evidentiary Standard
5.1 A finding must cite the snapshot date, HQLA amount, net cash outflows, and the computed LCR.
